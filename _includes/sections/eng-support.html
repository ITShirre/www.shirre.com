Benefits

Make the fixture invisible: PT-Flex and PT FCCL for test fixtures and calibration boards

Every test fixture adds its own loss, reflection and phase to what the VNA measures. The less the fixture adds, the less de-embedding has to remove, and the closer the result is to the DUT itself. PT-Flex and PT FCCL (PheroTetra®) are low-Dk, low-Df materials that keep the fixture out of the measurement.

  • Low loss: more of the measured loss belongs to the DUT.
    Low Df keeps the fixture's own insertion loss small, so more bandwidth stays usable and the DUT's real loss is easier to see.
  • Low, flat Dk: impedance and phase you can predict.
    Measured on published PheroTetra® striplines: PT150 Dkeff ≈ 2.36, flat across 1–49 GHz; hybrid Dkeff ~2.32, <1% over 5–63 GHz. Impedance is easier to hold, TRL line lengths are easier to calculate, and the same impedance allows a wider trace.
  • One material family from DUT to calibration standard.
    When the DUT, the test fixture and the TRL or 2x-Thru standards are all built on PheroTetra®, there is no material mismatch between them, so de-embedding is more reliable.
  • Smaller residual error, more repeatable results.
    A low-loss fixture with stable properties is easier to model, so less error remains after TRL or IEEE 370 2x-Thru de-embedding, and results stay consistent from calibration to measurement. Residuals also depend on the launch, connectors and layout of each design.
  • Flexible where rigid boards can't go.
    In a rigid-flex fixture, the rigid sections hold the SMA connectors (40 GHz) and the DUT connector firmly, while the flexible PT-Flex section routes, bends and conforms. This suits flex DUTs, bend tests and tight test setups that a rigid board cannot reach.

Measurement chain

From the 4-port VNA to the DUT

A 4-port VNA drives the differential pair (P / N) through two coaxial cables per side into SMA connectors (40 GHz) on the PheroTetra® test boards. The DUT plugs into a receptacle on each board. Coax calibration ends at the SMA; TRL or IEEE 370 2x-Thru de-embedding moves the reference plane to the DUT connector interface and removes test boards A and B.

Fixture loss share

How much of the measured loss is the fixture?

PheroTetra® has low loss, so a test fixture built on PT-Flex / PT FCCL takes up a smaller share of the measured loss and leaves more usable bandwidth. De-embedding has less to remove, and the measurement is more stable and easier to trust. Schematic illustration, not measured data.

Basic measurement equipment

RF / single-ended and differential measurements use the same core instruments. The port count and the test fixture change with the DUT.

  • 4Test fixture (measurement adapter board)
  • 5Device under test (DUT)
