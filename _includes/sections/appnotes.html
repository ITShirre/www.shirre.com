Development Case: Hybrid Board for a High-Density Optical Module

One Board, Folded to Fit: Hybrid Integration for a 300G-Class Optical Module

We developed a bendable hybrid board for a 12-channel optical module. One continuous flex section joined the optical-engine side and the electrical-connector side, so no board-to-board connector or cable was needed between them. The board was assembled flat, then folded once into a static bend, so the two rigid sections stacked vertically and freed space in a dense module. The design targeted a 300G-class module running 12 channels at 25 Gb/s.

{% endif %}

The challenge: more lanes, less room

A 12-channel optical module has to fit an optical engine, its electronics, and a high-density electrical connector into a compact space. Joining separate boards with a board-to-board connector or cable adds parts, height, and an assembly step.

Our approach: one hybrid board

We built the optics side and the electrical side as one board: two rigid sections joined by a continuous flex section. The flex section uses our PT-Flex (PheroTetra®) material. The board was populated while flat, then the flex was folded once into a static bend.

Advantages and features

  1. One board instead of three parts

    The optical-engine section and the connector section were joined by one continuous flex section, with no board-to-board connector or cable in between.

  2. Fold for vertical high-density integration

    Folding the flex stacked the two rigid sections vertically, freeing room for components in a compact module.

  3. Assemble flat, then fold once

    Components on both rigid sections faced the same side when the board was flat, so the board was populated while flat and then folded once into a static bend.

  4. Built for 12 lanes

    The board was designed for a 12-channel module at 25 Gb/s per channel, with 12 transmission-line pairs.

  5. PCBA + COB assembly

    The optical-engine section was assembled with a PCBA + chip-on-board (COB) process.

  6. Integrated electrical interface

    The second rigid section carried the electrical connector that linked the module to the host system.

Reference measurement

Measured on the same channel: optical modulation amplitude (OMA) of about 1.05 mW (about +0.2 dBm), within the 100GBASE-SR4 per-lane transmitter OMA range, and an extinction ratio above the 100GBASE-SR4 minimum of 2 dB. These are reference values from a development-stage lab sample, not a compliance measurement under IEEE 802.3 Clause 95 conditions.

The optical eye reflects the complete module and test setup, not the board alone.

PCIe Gen5/Gen6 for Edge Computing

Integrated paddle card + PT-Flex transmission line

A one-piece board that integrates the paddle card with the PT-Flex transmission line, so the gold fingers are formed directly on the flex. The design is integrated with a BTB (board-to-board) connector.

  • Paddle card and transmission line formed as one integrated board
  • Gold fingers made directly on PT-Flex

  • Integrated with a BTB (board-to-board) connector for 80+ pin high-speed lines
  • Total thickness 0.29 mm
  • Supports PCIe Gen5 up to 380 mm
