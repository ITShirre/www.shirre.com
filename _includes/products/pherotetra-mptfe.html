Core technology

Why modified PTFE

Our core competence is the dielectric itself. Shirre develops its own mPTFE formulations, turns them into FCCL grades and a matching bonding film, and builds those into flexible high-speed transmission lines.

Df 0.0009

Low loss

The 150 µm H type reaches Df 0.0009 for ultra-low insertion loss.

Dk 2.2–2.38

Low, stable Dk

Low Dk on the high-bandwidth grades supports faster signal propagation; measured PT150 stripline shows a flat Dk across frequency.

25–150 µm

Thin & flexible

Dielectric from 25 to 150 µm with 12 or 18 µm copper, including high-bendable S and F types for dynamic routing.

Applications & integration

From laminate to a complete high-speed link

PheroTetra® FCCL is in mass production across multiple high-bandwidth transmission applications. We pair it with the right connector for each interface specification, so you can source a complete flex interconnect, not only the laminate.

Where it is used

  • 112G / 224G SerDesHigh-speed links inside and between systems
  • PCIe Gen6Low-loss flex for server and accelerator I/O
  • 5G / mmWaveLow-Dk, low-Df RF and antenna feed lines
  • Thin flexible jumpers & cablesSpace-constrained routing where coax or twinax is too bulky

What we integrate

  • Connectors matched to the spec. A range of connector options to meet different interface and transmission requirements.
  • Active components on flex. Redrivers or ICs can be mounted directly on the flexible transmission line.
  • Stack-up design. Combine FCCL grades and PTNC16R bonding film, such as the PT100UE + PT75HE hybrid, to balance loss, thickness and cost.
  • Backed by measurement. VNA-measured insertion loss, return loss and TDR, with eye diagrams derived from the measured S-parameters.
