<{{ m7_h2 }} class="m7-h2">Dual-length method

Two assembly lengths; the fixed launch cancels in ΔIL / ΔL, isolating PT-Flex transmission-line loss α.

  • Goal: Isolate PT-Flex line-body loss α via ΔIL / ΔL.

  • α @ 15 GHz: 0.265 dB/cm (line body).

  • C2C jumper: an 8 cm run uses under 2 dB of the channel at 12.8 GHz.
  • I/O jumper: a 14 cm run stays near 3.2 dB at 12.8 GHz.
  • Linear budget: line-body IL = 0.226 dB/cm × L at 12.8 GHz.

How it is computed: Softboard-only IL = α × L, with α from the 200 / 300 mm ΔL slope — 0.226 dB/cm @ 12.8 GHz, 0.265 dB/cm @ 15 GHz. USB4-class Type-C test males cancel in ΔL and are not part of this reach. Softboard line body only; board launch and connectors add to this. Linear model from measured 200/300 mm ΔL; 2.5–15 GHz only.
